About Me

I am a researcher with a PhD in Chemistry from the University of Turku, Finland, where my doctoral thesis was approved with honours. My research focuses on electrochemistry, functional materials, conducting polymers, and their applications in electrochromic and energy-storage technologies.

My doctoral research explored the design and electrochemical synthesis of porphyrin-based electroactive copolymers, with particular emphasis on their optical, electrochemical, and energy-storage properties. My work combines materials synthesis, thin-film fabrication, electrochemical characterization, and in situ spectroscopic techniques to investigate the properties and behaviour of functional materials.

I am particularly interested in developing advanced functional materials that bridge fundamental electrochemical research and practical applications in energy storage, electrochromic devices, and sustainable technologies.
